MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2025
by and between NovaTech Solutions Inc. ("Disclosing Party") and BrightPath Consulting LLC
("Receiving Party").

1. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party agrees to hold in confidence all non-public information disclosed by the other
party, including business plans, customer lists, and technical specifications. Confidential
information shall not be disclosed to third parties without prior written consent.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
If either party engages the other for paid professional services under a separate statement
of work, invoices are due within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest
at 1.5% per month. All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes.

3. LIABILITY
Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising
from this Agreement. Total cumulative liability for direct damages shall not exceed the
greater of fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) or the fees paid in the twelve months preceding
the claim.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days written notice. Upon
termination, each party must return or destroy confidential materials within ten (10) business
days. Sections on confidentiality and liability survive termination.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict
of law principles.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
